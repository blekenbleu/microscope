Nikon Eclipse LV100

LV100 are upright episcopic and diascopic Nikon Industrial Microscopes, while LV150 are episcopic only.
LV100N POL come as either diascopic-only (lacking LV-UEPI-N vertical illuminator) or epi+diascopic.
LV100 infinity compound microscopes evolved from Optiphot 100, in turn based on Optiphot 2,
  popular for silicon wafer inspection.
